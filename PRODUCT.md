# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Online shoppers deciding whether to buy an item of clothing without trying it on. They land on the marketing site from search, ads, or social, evaluating whether the tool is trustworthy enough to use before checkout. The job to be done: see themselves (or a realistic model) in the outfit before spending money, to avoid buyer's remorse and returns.

## Product Purpose

Outfit AI generates realistic AI outfit visualizations so shoppers can preview how clothing will look before buying. The landing page is the primary surface — it must sell trust and quality in seconds, since the product's whole value proposition rests on the AI output looking convincing. Success is a visitor who believes the tool works and signs up.

## Brand Personality

Premium & editorial. Confident, fashion-forward, restrained — closer to a fashion magazine or a premium retailer (Net-a-Porter, SSENSE) than a SaaS dashboard. Bold, considered typography and generous whitespace over decorative chrome. Copy and visuals should feel curated, not templated.

## Anti-references

Explicitly avoid the generic AI-SaaS template look: gradient-clipped hero text, purple/indigo default palettes, identical icon-card grids, tiny uppercase eyebrows above every section, hero-metric blocks. If it looks like every other 2025-26 AI launch page, it's failed — this should read as fashion-editorial first, tech second.

## Design Principles

- Editorial confidence over SaaS cliché — borrow from fashion media layout conventions, not startup templates.
- Let the outfit imagery carry the page — photography and generated visuals are the proof, not icons or stock illustration.
- Restraint over decoration — one strong accent color, generous whitespace, no gratuitous gradients or glass effects.
- Trust through clarity — the "risk-free / preview before checkout" promise should read as confident and specific, never hand-wavy.
- Accessible by default — WCAG AA throughout; this is a consumer-facing storefront-adjacent surface, not an internal tool.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all scroll/reveal animations. No unusual constraints beyond AA.
